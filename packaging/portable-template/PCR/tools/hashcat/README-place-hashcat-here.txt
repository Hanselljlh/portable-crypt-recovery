Place Hashcat Here
==================

Place the Hashcat executable in this folder:

  Windows: hashcat.exe
  Linux:   hashcat  (or hashcat.bin)

Download Hashcat from:
  https://hashcat.net/hashcat/

PCR will automatically find Hashcat in this folder when the application starts.

After placing Hashcat here:
1. Launch PCR
2. Go to Settings
3. Click "Verify" to confirm Hashcat is working
4. Click "Scan Devices" to detect compute devices

Notes:
- PCR requires Hashcat 6.x or later
- You may also use Hashcat from your system PATH instead
- Hashcat is NOT bundled with PCR and must be obtained separately
